# UI/UX app benh nhan

## Dinh huong UI

- Phong cach: clear, sach, de dung cho ca nguoi lon tuoi.
- Mau chu dao: xanh la cay.
- Nen dung nen trang/xam rat nhat, chu den/xam dam, xanh la cho nut chinh va trang thai tich cuc.
- Han che man hinh qua nhieu chu. Moi man hinh nen co mot hanh dong chinh ro rang.
- Dung dashboard o trang chu va bottom navigation nhieu tab.

## Dieu huong tong the

### Bottom tabs de xuat

1. **Trang chu**
2. **Dat lich**
3. **Ket qua**
4. **Suc khoe**
5. **Tai khoan**

### Dashboard trang chu

Trang chu la noi gom cac tac vu quan trong nhat:

- The lich hen sap toi.
- Nut Dat lich kham noi bat.
- QR check-in neu co lich trong ngay.
- So thu tu neu da check-in.
- Thong bao ket qua moi.
- Don thuoc/nhac uong thuoc hom nay.
- Shortcut: Ket qua, Thanh toan, Ho so, Tu van, Goi kham.

## Luong 0. Onboarding va dang nhap

### Man hinh

- Splash.
- Onboarding ngan.
- Dang nhap bang so dien thoai.
- Nhap OTP.
- Lien ket ho so benh nhan.
- Tao PIN/bat sinh trac hoc.

### Luong di

1. Mo app.
2. Neu chua dang nhap, vao dang nhap.
3. Nhap so dien thoai.
4. Nhap OTP.
5. Neu tim thay ho so tren HIS/EMR, hien man lien ket.
6. Neu chua co ho so, cho tao ho so moi.
7. Vao dashboard.

## Luong 1. Trang chu dashboard

### Man hinh

- Dashboard.
- Trung tam thong bao.
- Chon ho so dang xem.

### Thanh phan UI

- Header: chao benh nhan, avatar, icon thong bao.
- Selector ho so: ban than/nguoi than.
- Card lich hen sap toi.
- Card so thu tu/check-in.
- Grid shortcut 2 cot hoac 4 icon ngang.
- Section ket qua moi.
- Section nhac viec hom nay.

### Trang thai can co

- Chua co lich hen.
- Co lich hen sap toi.
- Da check-in.
- Co ket qua moi.
- Loi tai du lieu.

## Luong 2. Dat lich kham

### Man hinh

- Chon nguoi kham.
- Chon chuyen khoa/dich vu.
- Chon bac si neu co.
- Chon ngay gio.
- Nhap ly do kham.
- Xac nhan lich hen.
- Dat lich thanh cong.

### Luong di

1. Tu tab Dat lich hoac shortcut tren dashboard.
2. Chon ho so benh nhan.
3. Chon chuyen khoa/dich vu.
4. Chon bac si hoac bo qua neu benh vien xep bac si sau.
5. Chon ngay gio con trong.
6. Nhap ly do kham.
7. Xac nhan.
8. Quay ve chi tiet lich hen.

## Luong 3. Lich hen, check-in va hang cho

### Man hinh

- Danh sach lich hen.
- Chi tiet lich hen.
- QR check-in.
- So thu tu realtime.
- Doi/huy lich.

### Luong di

1. Benh nhan mo lich hen.
2. Chon lich hen sap toi.
3. Neu den ngay kham, hien QR check-in.
4. Sau khi check-in, hien so thu tu.
5. Khi gan den luot, gui push notification.

## Luong 4. Ket qua kham

### Man hinh

- Danh sach lan kham.
- Chi tiet lan kham.
- Ket qua xet nghiem.
- Chan doan hinh anh/PDF.
- Don thuoc va loi dan.

### Luong di

1. Vao tab Ket qua.
2. Chon ho so benh nhan.
3. Chon lan kham.
4. Xem ket qua tong quan.
5. Mo chi tiet xet nghiem/PDF/don thuoc.

## Luong 5. Don thuoc va nhac uong thuoc

### Man hinh

- Danh sach don thuoc.
- Chi tiet don thuoc.
- Cai dat nhac uong thuoc.
- Lich uong hom nay.

### Luong di

1. Tu ket qua kham hoac tab Suc khoe.
2. Mo don thuoc.
3. Xem cach dung.
4. Bat nhac uong thuoc.
5. Xac nhan da uong tren notification hoac trong app.

## Luong 6. Thanh toan

### Man hinh

- Khoan can thanh toan.
- Chi tiet hoa don.
- Chon phuong thuc thanh toan.
- Ket qua thanh toan.
- Lich su giao dich.

### Luong di

1. Tu dashboard/lich hen/ket qua.
2. Xem khoan can thanh toan.
3. Chon phuong thuc.
4. Thanh toan.
5. Nhan bien lai va cap nhat trang thai.

## Luong 7. Suc khoe ca nhan

### Man hinh

- Tab Suc khoe.
- Nhap chi so.
- Bieu do chi so.
- Ho so y te cu.
- Cham soc sau kham.
- So tiem chung.

### Luong di

1. Vao tab Suc khoe.
2. Chon nhap chi so, xem bieu do, upload ho so, vaccine hoac cham soc sau kham.
3. Moi module co man chi tiet rieng.
4. Cac nhac viec quan trong quay lai dashboard.

## Luong 8. Tu van truc tuyen

### Man hinh

- Danh sach hoi thoai.
- Tao yeu cau tu van.
- Chat chi tiet.
- Gui anh/file.

### Luong di

1. Tu dashboard hoac Tai khoan.
2. Tao yeu cau tu van.
3. Chon van de/chuyen khoa.
4. Gui noi dung.
5. Nhan phan hoi va push notification.

## Luong 9. Goi kham va dich vu

### Man hinh

- Danh sach goi kham.
- Chi tiet goi.
- Dang ky goi.
- Goi da mua/da dang ky.

### Luong di

1. Tu dashboard shortcut.
2. Xem danh sach goi.
3. Mo chi tiet.
4. Dang ky hoac dat lich theo goi.

## Luong 10. Tai khoan va bao mat

### Man hinh

- Tab Tai khoan.
- Ho so ca nhan.
- Ho so gia dinh.
- Cai dat thong bao.
- Bao mat va dong y.
- Danh gia dich vu.
- Ho tro.

### Luong di

1. Vao tab Tai khoan.
2. Chon thong tin can quan ly.
3. Cap nhat ho so, them nguoi than, doi cai dat thong bao hoac cau hinh bao mat.

## Quy tac UI de app clear

- Moi man hinh chi nen co 1 CTA chinh.
- Dashboard khong nen hien qua 6 shortcut dau tien; cac chuc nang con lai dua vao "Xem tat ca".
- Text ngan, uu tien label de hieu: Dat lich, Ket qua, Don thuoc, Thanh toan.
- Dung icon quen thuoc, nhung icon-only phai co label.
- Card lich hen va so thu tu phai noi bat nhat tren dashboard.
- Thong tin y te nhay cam khong hien day du tren notification.
- Tat ca list can co empty state than thien.
- Tat ca API screen can co loading, error, retry.
